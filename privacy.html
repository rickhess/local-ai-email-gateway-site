Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Purpose

Local AI Email Gateway is a personal desktop application used by its operator to retrieve and classify Gmail messages.

Information Accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the application accesses Gmail message content and metadata using Google's Gmail API.

How Information Is Used

Gmail information is used to classify messages by sensitivity and support personal email organization. Classification is performed locally on the operator's computer using locally running software and AI models.

Storage and Sharing

The application may temporarily store message content and retain classification records on the operator's computer. Gmail information is not published on this website or sold to third parties.

Data Protection

The application uses local access controls and process isolation to help protect stored information. Access to Gmail requires authorization from the account owner.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local message copies and classification records can be deleted from the operator's computer. Gmail data remains subject to the account owner's settings and Google's retention policies.

Revoking Access

The account owner can revoke the application's Gmail access through their Google Account security settings.

Contact

This application is maintained for personal use. Questions about its privacy practices can be directed to the application's developer through the contact information displayed on Google's OAuth consent screen.
